# Liora branding

The supplied gold-and-ivory floral logo is traced into real SVG paths, with no embedded JPEG or other raster image. The tracing approximates the original image's metallic shading and fine floral details.

- `public/assets/liora-wedding-logo.svg`: detailed logo used on authentication pages; also suitable for downloading and scaling.
- `public/assets/liora-logo-mark.svg`: smaller vector version used in the site header, dashboard and browser icon metadata.
- `src/app/favicon.ico` and `src/app/apple-icon.png`: browser and home-screen icons based on the supplied artwork.

Brand labels, page metadata, contact message text and Free Plan credits use **Liora**. The supplied tagline, “Where Love Becomes Forever,” and romantic invitation wording retain their original meaning. Existing session cookies and saved-draft keys remain compatible with previously saved invitations.

To replace the logo later, keep these filenames or update their references in `SiteHeader.tsx`, `AuthShell.tsx`, the dashboard layout and the root metadata.
