# Rosé Atelier floral cover artwork

The reference-inspired cover uses a photographic crimson damask envelope, burgundy/pink flower borders and ivory paper. It adds warm ambient light, 18 small sparkles, two gently floating butterflies and the existing falling petals. The welcome keeps only the small invitation heading above the envelope; the guest name and personalized message remain below it. The raster cover fades into the working CSS flap and sliding letter on opening. All decorations ignore clicks and follow the animation setting and reduced-motion preference.

The opened invitation now continues the same floral paper throughout the cover, story, events, galleries, film and RSVP. Translucent stationery panels keep text readable over the flowers. `PremiumFallingPetals.tsx` adds 72 softly shaded rose petals distributed across nine vertical bands, so petals continue falling as guests scroll; mobile shows 54. Change `PETAL_COUNT` to adjust density and `PremiumFallingPetals.module.css` to adjust speed or shape. The effect disappears while the film is open or decorative motion is disabled, and respects the system reduced-motion preference. Background artwork blends with custom paper colours, including dark palettes.

## Final assets and implementation

- `public/assets/premium/rose-envelope.webp` — transparent embossed red envelope with red wax seal; about 350 KB.
- `public/assets/premium/rose-floral-paper.webp` — blush/ivory floral background; about 199 KB.
- `src/components/premium/PremiumWelcome.tsx` — the welcome screen, envelope image, guest message and decorative elements.
- `src/components/premium/PremiumInvitation.module.css` — welcome layout, layer transitions, sparkle/butterfly movement and responsive rules.

The built-in **image_gen** tool generated both original PNGs. Sharp converted them to WebP for delivery, preserving the envelope's alpha channel. Original generated PNGs remain in the generator's default directory; the application uses only the optimized workspace assets. The fixed photographic colours follow the supplied crimson/floral reference; customer palette controls continue to theme the paper, text, working opening layers and invitation sections. The artwork blends with the paper colour, including darker themes. Faint floral texture also appears behind the story and RSVP sections. The Premium package card shows the new envelope.
